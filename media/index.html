02 · Founder

Founder Information &
Official Quote

The following materials are approved for use in media coverage. Please do not alter the quote.

Hi-res photo
Ernesto Dwehsidi Herbert
Founder · Alkebuleum Foundation

Ernesto Dwehsidi Herbert is the founder of the Alkebuleum Foundation and the architect of the Alkebuleum Trust Ecosystem. A Pan-African technologist and entrepreneur, Herbert holds a Master's degree in Data Science from George Washington University and brings more than 15 years of experience building data infrastructure — the systems institutions depend on to record truth, protect information, and verify trust at scale.

Herbert developed the Alkebuleum concept from a foundational insight: that Africa's economic and governance challenges are not primarily problems of resources or will, but problems of trust infrastructure. His work spans blockchain protocol design, institutional outreach, and the strategic alignment of Alkebuleum with continental frameworks including the AfCFTA and the African Union's Agenda 2063. The 2022 Alkebuleum whitepaper, which he authored, laid the foundation for what is now a multi-protocol trust ecosystem serving governments, institutions, and the global African diaspora.

"A blockchain is only as trusted as those who validate it. Alkebuleum's validators are the institutions Africa already trusts."

03 · Approved Descriptions

Boilerplate Copy &
Approved Language

Use these descriptions verbatim in coverage. They are approved for publication without further clearance.

One-sentence description
Alkebuleum is a Layer 1 public, permissioned blockchain purpose-built for the African continent and its global diaspora, serving as the sovereign trust infrastructure for identity, institutional records, payments, credit, and governance.
Short description (2–3 sentences)
Alkebuleum is a sovereign Layer 1 blockchain built for Africa and the global African diaspora. It is secured by PORA — Proof of Reputation and Authority — a consensus mechanism in which validators are exclusively reputable institutions including central banks, universities, government agencies, fintechs, and diaspora organizations. The network provides trust infrastructure for identity, institutional records, payments, and credit across the African continent and beyond.
Council description
The Alkebuleum Council is the body of reputable institutions that govern, validate, and shape the Alkebuleum network. Its members include central banks, commercial banks, universities, government ministries, regulators, fintech companies, civil-society organizations, national archives, and diaspora institutions from across Africa and the global African family. The Council is currently forming its founding cohort, with four participation pathways available: Council Observer, Founding Council Member, Validator Partner, and Implementation Partner.
Full boilerplate (for press release ends)
About Alkebuleum: Alkebuleum is a Layer 1 public, permissioned blockchain built for the African continent and its global diaspora. Secured by PORA (Proof of Reputation and Authority), the network is validated by reputable institutions — central banks, universities, government agencies, fintechs, and diaspora organizations — rather than anonymous capital holders. Alkebuleum provides trust infrastructure for identity, institutional records, payments, and credit, and is aligned with the AfCFTA, the African Union's Agenda 2063, and the Digital Transformation Strategy for Africa 2020–2030. The Alkebuleum Foundation is the steward of the network and its ecosystem. For press enquiries, contact press@alkebuleum.org. Visit alkebuleum.org.
04 · Ecosystem

The Trust Ecosystem:
Component Summary

Each component of the Alkebuleum Trust Ecosystem in one sentence, approved for media use.

Component Category Description
Alkebuleum Council Governance The governing body of reputable African and diaspora institutions that validate, guide standards, and shape the direction of the Alkebuleum network.
AlkeLedger Core Protocol A blockchain-anchored institutional records ledger for documents, audit trails, certificates, and accountability across governments and organizations.
AlkePay Core Protocol Payment request links, collections, disbursements, and cross-border settlement infrastructure connected to the Alkebuleum trust layer.
AlkeID Core Protocol Zero-knowledge identity infrastructure for verifiable credentials, citizen IDs, institutional identity, and cross-border digital participation.
AlkeCredit Core Protocol Portable credit history and reputation infrastructure built from verified identity, payment records, and institutional participation data.
ALKE (Native Asset) Utility The native utility coin of the Alkebuleum network, used for transaction fees, validator rewards, and protocol participation.
JollofSwap Ecosystem App A decentralized exchange and liquidity application built on Alkebuleum for trading ecosystem assets.
Nuru Ecosystem App A user-facing wallet, assistant, and gateway application for everyday interaction with the Alkebuleum ecosystem.
DRIS Ecosystem App Digital Records Infrastructure Service — an application for national and institutional digital archives, land records, and document registries.
AfPass Ecosystem App An identity passport and access layer enabling trusted participation and credentialing across the Alkebuleum ecosystem.
