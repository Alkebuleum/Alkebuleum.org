05 — Usage Guidelines

You may

  • Use the logo and mark in editorial, news, and research coverage of Alkebuleum
  • Reference the brand name and colors in integrations, apps, and developer tools
  • Use assets in presentations, whitepapers, and academic work that reference Alkebuleum
  • Use the wordmark or mark at any size, provided it is not distorted

Please do not

  • Alter, recolor, or distort the logo or mark
  • Use the brand to imply official partnership or endorsement without written permission
  • Use the name "Alkebuleum" as part of your own product or company name
  • Reproduce assets in a way that could mislead or confuse users about the source
